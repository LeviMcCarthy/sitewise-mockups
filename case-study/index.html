The AI Quote System

Customer uploads a photo → gets a price in 30 seconds

No more manual estimates. The AI analyzes the photo, measures what it sees, and applies real pricing rates. Here's the flow:

1

Customer picks their services

Lawn mowing, window washing, gutter cleaning, hedge trimming — select one or multiple.

2

Enter address + upload a photo

They snap a photo of their property. The address helps the AI estimate lot size for lawns.

3

AI analyzes and quotes instantly

Counts windows, measures lawn area, checks gutter condition — applies your exact pricing rates.

4

Customer books on the spot

Happy with the price? They pick a date and book — you get an email notification immediately.
